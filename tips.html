Essential Travel Tips

Everything you need to know before you board your flight to Japan.

💴 Currency Information

The currency in Japan is the Japanese Yen (JPY). While credit cards are increasingly accepted, especially in major cities and hotels, Japan is still largely a cash-based society. It is highly recommended to carry cash for small purchases, local restaurants, and temples.

🚄 Transportation Guide

Japan boasts one of the most efficient public transport systems in the world. The Japan Rail (JR) Pass is highly recommended for tourists traveling between multiple cities. In cities like Tokyo, IC cards (Suica or Pasmo) make riding the subway seamless.

🙇 Travel Etiquette

Politeness is key. Bowing is the standard greeting. Always take off your shoes before entering someone's home, traditional ryokans, and some temples or restaurants. Tipping is not customary in Japan and can sometimes be considered rude.

🛡️ Safety Tips

Japan is considered one of the safest countries globally, with extremely low crime rates. However, you should still practice common sense. Be aware of natural disasters like earthquakes; familiarize yourself with evacuation routes at your accommodation.

🌸 Best Time to Visit

Spring (March to May) is famous for the stunning cherry blossoms (sakura). Autumn (September to November) offers spectacular red and gold foliage. Both are peak tourist seasons with mild, comfortable weather.

📱 Connectivity

Renting a pocket Wi-Fi or buying a tourist SIM card at the airport is highly recommended. While public Wi-Fi is available in major train stations and convenience stores, having a constant connection is vital for using navigation apps like Google Maps.
